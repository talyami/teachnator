# How to Train Yourself to Become Ridiculously Smart

If you want to train yourself to understand things faster and overall become smarter, here are four core habits you can practice:

---

### 1. Stop Consuming Information Without Retrieving It
After you learn something—close the book, video, or article—and ask yourself:
> *"What can I explain without looking?"*

- **Retrieval Practice:** Trying to pull information out of your brain strengthens your memory far more than repeatedly trying to put information back in.
- **In Practice:** Instead of rereading something five times, read it once, close the source, and try to recall as much as you can.
- **The Result:** Your brain gets better over time at accessing and using what it stores.

---

### 2. Understand the "Why"
Whenever someone gives you a fact, a rule, or an opinion, ask yourself:
> *"Why is this true?"*

- Ask *why* again, and keep digging until you reach foundational principles you actually understand.
- **Conclusions vs. Understanding:** Memorizing conclusions makes you knowledgeable, but understanding *how* a conclusion was reached makes you capable of producing your own.
- **Bottom-Up Thinking:** When you understand concepts from the bottom up, you become much harder to confuse.

---

### 3. Build Connections Between Seemingly Unrelated Things
Every time you encounter something new, ask yourself:
> *"What does this remind me of?"*

- The brain does not store knowledge as completely isolated facts.
- New information becomes significantly easier to comprehend and retain when anchored to existing knowledge and mental models.

---

### 4. Practice Compression
Take a complicated concept and progressively condense it:
1. **One paragraph**
2. **One sentence**
3. **One word**

- **Find the Core:** This forces your brain to separate the essential idea from the surrounding noise.
- **The Litmus Test:** If you can't simplify something without making it inaccurate, you probably don't understand it as well as you think you do.
